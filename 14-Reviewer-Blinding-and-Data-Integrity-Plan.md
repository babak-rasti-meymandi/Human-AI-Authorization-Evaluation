# Public Summary — Reviewer Blinding and Data Integrity

**Status:** Public boundary statement; detailed study controls are not disclosed here.

The public S01 scenario and its relevant test outcome are already disclosed. S01 is therefore not eligible for blinded confirmatory testing. It may be used only for explicitly labelled exploratory work, with its exposure limitations stated.

Any future confirmatory human-review study would require a separately designed and frozen study, a held-out scenario, verified access controls, documented reviewer assignment and exposure controls, appropriate consent and privacy arrangements, and a data-integrity procedure defined before recruitment. Non-public scenario identifiers, contents, investigator keys, packet structures, and allocation details are intentionally excluded from this public record.

No human reviewer has been recruited or assessed, and no human-review data have been collected. This document does not claim that a confirmatory study is ready, authorized, or underway.
