# Public Summary — Future Human-Review Study

**Status:** Planning summary only; no human study is currently authorized or underway.

This public file intentionally does not disclose non-public scenario identifiers, scenario content, investigator keys, packet allocation, reviewer assignment, or other materials that could compromise a future study.

Any future human-review study would require a separately designed and versioned protocol, a held-out scenario, verified access controls, appropriate consent and privacy arrangements, defined data-retention and AI-service-use terms, and an explicit authorization to begin recruitment. The protocol and materials must be frozen before data collection.

The publicly documented S01 scenario is not suitable for blinded confirmatory testing because its behavior and relevant test outcome are already public. It may support only clearly labelled exploratory work within the limits of its public exposure.

No reviewer recruitment, participation, or human assessment is claimed here. Any future findings must be reported according to the actual study design, sample, evidence, and limitations. This summary does not establish that a suitable held-out scenario or frozen protocol exists.
