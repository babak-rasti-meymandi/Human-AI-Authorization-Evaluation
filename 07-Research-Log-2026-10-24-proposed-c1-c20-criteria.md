# Research Log — Proposed C1–C20 Criteria Draft

**Date:** 2026-10-24  
**Status:** Draft created; not frozen and not validated  
**Repository:** Human-AI Authorization Evaluation

## Trigger

The researcher confirmed that no separate authoritative C1–C20 table is available and asked whether the criteria would be produced. The project therefore needs a transparent new proposal rather than indefinite waiting for a missing source.

## Work completed

Created `12-Proposed-Criteria-C1-C20.md`, a new 20-criterion draft derived from the public research question, synthetic S01 case, evidence register, and project distinctions among intent, semantic deviation, authorization, technical correctness, and evidence sufficiency.

The criteria cover:
- identification of intent, scope, baseline, intended outcome, and decision authority;
- identification and assessment of semantic change;
- disclosure, specificity, informed and affirmative authorization, authority, scope, timing, amendment, and revocation;
- evidence provenance, linkage, chronology, and calibrated conclusions.

Updated the README and task list to link the new draft and make review of this proposal the next gate. Updated the external-framework crosswalk template to reference the proposed criterion names without claiming that any criterion has already been mapped to NIST or ISO.

## Methodological cautions

1. The new criteria are **not** presented as a recovered or preserved historical version of any previous C1–C20 set.
2. The criteria are not validated measurement instruments and are not frozen or preregistered.
3. No formal reviewer recruitment or evaluation has occurred.
4. NIST and ISO mappings remain pending. Each mapping must be grounded in an exact public source reference; licensed or otherwise inaccessible ISO text must not be guessed.
5. The set must be reviewed for overlap, observability, neutrality, and applicability to both S01-A and S01-B before formal data collection.
6. No BAES repository was modified. No private BAES Foundation wording was added to the public research repository.
