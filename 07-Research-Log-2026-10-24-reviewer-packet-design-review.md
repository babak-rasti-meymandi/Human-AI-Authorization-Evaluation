# Research Log — 2026-10-24: Reviewer Packet Design Review

## Review performed

After the reviewer-packet files were added to the public repository, the current protocol, both condition instructions, the recording sheet, packet manifest, evidence register, and research log were inspected for internal consistency.

## Methodological issue identified

The earlier Condition A instruction and the shared recording sheet explicitly asked reviewers to analyze intent alignment, authorization, and evidence sufficiency. Those prompts overlapped substantially with Condition B's structured instrument and could reduce the contrast between conditions.

## Decision proposed

Revise Condition A to use a neutral, conventional code-review request: overall recommendation, supporting evidence, concerns, and additional information requested. Condition B uses the same neutral response task and the same underlying artifacts, plus the structured prompts. This change is intended to compare the addition of explicit structured prompts without depriving Condition A of ordinary engineering evidence.

## Boundary-test disclosure policy

For the default reviewer packet, the boundary-test source and result remain investigator-side and are not provided to reviewers in either authorization variant or either condition. The supplied code and characterization tests are the same in all cells. Reviewers can still inspect the code and may independently notice the semantic difference or ask for additional tests. If this policy changes, update all packet variants consistently and record a protocol amendment before collecting formal responses.

## Proposed assignment and reporting

A draft pre-registration plan is being added for a small 2 × 2 between-reviewer design: Condition A or B crossed with S01-A or S01-B. The preferred target is eight reviewers (two per cell); four is a limited feasibility-pilot minimum, not a basis for statistical generalization. Each reviewer should receive one packet only. Results should be reported descriptively, without a composite score or claims of statistical significance.

## Status and limitations

These are constructed stimuli. No reviewer recruitment or formal human review has occurred. The protocol and instruments remain drafts until reviewed, versioned, and explicitly frozen. The proposed plan is not yet registered or frozen. No finding about reviewer performance or the superiority of either condition is available.

## Confidentiality

No private BAES material or personal source code was added to these public artifacts. The research remains independent of BAES normative repositories; the BAES Foundation remains unchanged.
