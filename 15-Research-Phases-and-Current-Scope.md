# Research Phases and Current Scope

**Decision date:** 2026-11-02  
**Status:** Phase 1 is the active research scope; Phase 2 is the next planned phase and is deferred.  
**Research status:** Design and evidence-packet construction only. No human reviewer has been recruited or provided assistance, and no human assessment has been conducted.

## 1. Purpose of this decision

This document separates two related but methodologically distinct objectives. The immediate work concerns the structure, sufficiency, provenance, and reconstructability of evidence records. A later phase will examine how human reviewers interpret those records and distinguish approval from informed authorization.

The separation prevents the project from treating an unperformed human study as completed, or from using reviewer-performance results to answer a prior documentary question.

## 2. Phase 1 — Evidence-record reconstructability and sufficiency

**This is the active phase.**

### Research objective

Determine whether a controlled evidence packet, considered on its own documented contents, provides a traceable basis to reconstruct:

- the original human instruction and its applicable scope;
- the proposed and recorded engineering change;
- the observable semantic difference;
- the relevant human decision, if documented;
- the authority basis and scope for that decision, if documented;
- the relationship between authorization records and execution records;
- unresolved evidence gaps and residual ambiguity.

The unit of assessment is the evidence packet and its records—not a human reviewer's skill, judgment, speed, or consistency.

### Method

1. Stabilize the public synthetic C99 baseline, proposed variant, and available test evidence.
2. Construct clearly labelled synthetic controlled evidence variants, including an explicit-authorization case and an approval-without-established-authorization case.
3. Keep common technical artifacts identical across variants; vary only the pre-specified authorization evidence.
4. Build an evidence index with artifact identifiers, provenance, timestamps, actors/sources, versions, and the claims each artifact supports.
5. Map each C1–C20 criterion to the evidence available, recording supported, contradicted, not established, ambiguous, or not applicable as appropriate.
6. Perform an investigator-side traceability and reconstruction audit: identify which event/authority links can be reconstructed from the packet and which cannot.
7. Record residual ambiguity, alternative interpretations, assumptions avoided, and additional records that would be required.
8. Preserve the distinction between constructed evidence and historical or real-world events.

### What Phase 1 can and cannot establish

Phase 1 can establish whether the packet is internally coherent, traceable, sufficiently documented for the specified reconstruction task, and explicit about its limitations.

Without an independent human reviewer, Phase 1 **cannot** establish that independent reviewers actually interpret the packet correctly, consistently, or efficiently. The investigator-side audit is not a substitute for human validation and must not be reported as such.

No reviewer-performance comparison, conventional-versus-structured review comparison, or empirical claim about human detection is part of Phase 1.

## 3. Phase 2 — Human reviewer interpretation and performance

**This is the direct next phase after Phase 1; it is deferred, not cancelled.**

### Research objective

Examine how human reviewers, using only assigned evidence packets, distinguish:

- technical correctness from alignment with the original instruction;
- artifact approval from informed authorization of a specific semantic deviation;
- established facts from unsupported inference and unresolved evidence gaps.

A further exploratory question may compare conventional review with a structured recording aid, provided the protocol and treatment difference are finalized in advance.

### Entry conditions

Phase 2 may begin only when:

- Phase 1's packet design and evidence index have been reviewed and stabilized;
- an appropriate human reviewer or reviewer group is available;
- the held-out scenario and alternate packets are stored under verified access controls;
- reviewer instructions, coding rules, assignment, exposure handling, consent/privacy arrangements, and data retention are defined;
- the protocol and materials are explicitly versioned and frozen before recruitment.

The existing public S01 scenario is not eligible for blinded confirmatory testing because its substantive behavior and boundary-test result are public. It may be used only for clearly labelled exploratory usability work. Any confirmatory human study requires a new held-out scenario.

## 4. Relationship between the phases

| Dimension | Phase 1 — Active | Phase 2 — Deferred |
|---|---|---|
| Primary object | Evidence packet and records | Human interpretation of assigned packet |
| Main question | Can the record support a traceable reconstruction? | Can reviewers correctly and consistently interpret the record? |
| Human participants | Not required for the investigator-side documentary audit | Required |
| Main outputs | Evidence index, C1–C20 evidence mapping, reconstruction audit, ambiguity register | Preserved reviewer responses, coded outcomes, disagreement and limitations |
| Claims permitted | Documentary sufficiency and limits of the examined packet | Descriptive claims about observed reviewer responses, bounded by sample and design |
| Claims not permitted | Human performance, reviewer consistency, or method superiority | General population effects or universal superiority from a small exploratory study |

## 5. Evidence and reporting safeguards

- Synthetic evidence must be labelled as constructed; it is not a historical event record.
- An absent record in a packet does not prove that the event never occurred elsewhere.
- A PR approval does not by itself establish informed authorization for a disclosed semantic deviation.
- Test success does not by itself establish semantic equivalence or authorization.
- Do not infer AI observation, reasoning, proposal, or execution without attributable AI interaction records.
- Keep framework text coverage, event evidence sufficiency, and residual ambiguity separate.
- Do not create a composite score without a justified, pre-specified method.
- Do not publish private BAES material or internal derivations.
- No human-review result, confirmatory result, or comparative-performance claim may be reported before the relevant Phase 2 work is actually completed.

## 6. Current decision

The active work is Phase 1 only. Phase 2 remains the immediate planned successor and must not be silently dropped, merged into Phase 1, or described as already underway.

This decision changes the research sequence and scope; it does not freeze the protocol, validate the criteria, complete the evidence packets, or authorize a human study.
