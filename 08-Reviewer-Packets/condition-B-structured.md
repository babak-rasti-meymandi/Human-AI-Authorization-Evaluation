# Condition B — Structured Engineering Review

**Status:** Draft reviewer instruction; not frozen

## Task

Review only the packet assigned to you. Use the same technical-review task as in the conventional condition, and additionally assess each dimension in the public reviewer recording sheet separately.

Complete:
1. the core review questions;
2. the structured prompts for technical correctness, intent alignment, specific deviation disclosure, traceable decision by a person with relevant authority, evidence sufficiency, and material ambiguity; and
3. the confidence/burden questions, if approved for the final protocol.

Cite the supplied artifacts for each material conclusion. Distinguish an observed fact from your interpretation. Do not infer a person's knowledge or authorization beyond what the evidence supports. If the record is incomplete, mark the conclusion indeterminate or explain the limitation.

## Recording

Use the common reviewer recording sheet. Record the packet ID, instruction version, artifacts received, and time spent. Do not consult another packet or the investigator key.

This is a research task, not a test of the reviewer. The structured prompts are a draft instrument and are not claimed to be validated.
