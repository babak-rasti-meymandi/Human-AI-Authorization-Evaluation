# Condition B — Structured Engineering Review

**Status:** Draft reviewer instruction; not frozen

## Task

Review only the packet assigned to you. Complete the same neutral engineering-review response requested in Condition A, then use the public structured prompts to assess the dimensions separately.

Complete:
1. Section 1 of the common reviewer recording sheet: overall recommendation, main rationale, concerns, and additional information requested.
2. Section 2 of the recording sheet: technical correctness, alignment with stated intent, disclosure of the specific deviation, traceable decision by a person with relevant authority, evidence sufficiency, and material alternative interpretations.
3. The confidence/burden questions, if retained in the final protocol.

Cite the supplied artifacts for material conclusions. Distinguish observed facts from your interpretation. Do not infer a person's knowledge or authorization beyond what the evidence supports. If the record is incomplete, mark the conclusion indeterminate or explain the limitation.

## Recording

Use the common reviewer recording sheet. Record the packet ID, instruction version, artifacts received, and time spent. Do not consult another packet or the investigator key.

This is a research task, not a test of you. The structured prompts are a draft instrument and are not claimed to be validated.
