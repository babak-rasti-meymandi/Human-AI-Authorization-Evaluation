## Criteria

| ID | Criterion | Principal question |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | Original Human Intent | Can the original instruction be reconstructed accurately? |
| C2 | Intent Boundary | Is the permitted and prohibited scope of change explicit? |
| C3 | Delegated Authority | Is the agent’s delegated authority identifiable? |
| C4 | Authority Scope | Does the delegation cover the specific action taken? |
| C5 | AI Observation | Can the observation that prompted the change be distinguished from interpretation? |
| C6 | AI Reasoning | Is the reasoning recorded and attributable, without treating its existence as proof of correctness? |
| C7 | AI Proposed Action | Can the proposed action be distinguished from the executed action? |
| C8 | AI Executed Action | Can the actual action and its provenance be established? |
| C9 | Evidence | Are relevant evidence items identifiable, attributable, and linked to the claims they support? |
| C10 | Human Review | What exactly did the human review, and what did the review establish? |
| C11 | Human Decision | Is there an identifiable human decision rather than an inferred decision? |
| C12 | Decision Scope | Is the scope of the human decision explicit? |
| C13 | Authorization Change | Was authorization amended by an appropriately authorized actor? |
| C14 | Execution | Can execution, timing, and resulting artifact or deployment be established? |
| C15 | Intent Deviation | Can the deviation from the original instruction be identified and characterized? |
| C16 | Deviation Acceptance | Is acceptance of the deviation explicit, attributable, and authorized? |
| C17 | Revocation / Amendment | Can the applicable authorization state, including relevant amendments or revocations, be reconstructed at execution time? |
| C18 | Traceability | Can the relevant records be linked into a coherent, time-ordered chain? |
| C19 | Actor Attribution | Can actions and decisions be attributed to the responsible actors? |
| C20 | Unambiguous Reconstruction | Can an independent reviewer reconstruct the relevant events, authority, decisions, and outcome without material ambiguity? |
