# Invitation — Independent Second-Coder Review

**Use:** Draft invitation for an individual researcher or practitioner with relevant experience in AI governance, software engineering, empirical software engineering, requirements engineering, or standards evaluation.

## Suggested subject

Request for Independent Second-Coder Review — Human Authorization in AI-Assisted Engineering

## Email draft

Dear [Name],

I am conducting an independent, scenario-based research project examining how authorization and semantic deviations are evidenced in AI-assisted software engineering.

I have completed an initial investigator-side documentary analysis comparing selected public BAES formulations with the NIST AI Risk Management Framework 1.0, and examining two controlled synthetic evidence packets. The work is deliberately bounded: it does not claim framework superiority, and it treats the ISO/IEC 42001:2023 clause-level mapping as unreviewed where the final published text has not been available for examination.

I am now seeking an independent second coder to assess whether the documentary coding can be reproduced from the underlying sources and evidence, without first seeing my ratings or rationales.

The requested review would cover:

1. Framework-text coverage (T0–T3) for the recovered historical C1–C20 evaluation questions, using public BAES materials and the NIST AI RMF 1.0 Core; ISO/IEC 42001:2023 should be marked unreviewed/access-limited where its authorized final clause text is unavailable.
2. Event-evidence sufficiency (R0–R3) for two short synthetic S01 evidence packets.
3. A concise rationale and exact source locator for each rating, including disagreements, uncertainty, and evidence gaps.

The review package includes the protocol, the recovered criteria, source links, the synthetic evidence artifacts, and a blank coding worksheet. It does not require access to private BAES material, confidential repositories, or licensed ISO text. The scenarios are explicitly synthetic and do not represent real organizational events.

Independence is important: I ask that the reviewer complete and date the worksheet before seeing the investigator's current coding. Disagreement is welcome and will be preserved rather than edited away. Participation would not imply endorsement of BAES or of the research conclusions. No public attribution or use of your name would occur without your explicit permission.

Would you be willing to consider this review? If the topic is within your expertise, I would be glad to send the review package and agree a reasonable scope, schedule, and any applicable fee or terms before work begins. If it is outside your area, a referral to a suitable independent researcher would also be appreciated.

Thank you for considering the request.

Kind regards,

Babak Rasti M.  
Independent Researcher — Human–AI Engineering  
Project: BAES Pilot Evaluation  
Research repository: https://github.com/babak-rasti-meymandi/Human-AI-Authorization-Evaluation

## Sending guidance

- Personalize the first sentence to the recipient's actual research area.
- Prefer researchers with demonstrable methods experience over seniority or title alone.
- Do not attach the investigator's current coding, calibration review, audit, or synthesis.
- Do not send BAES-SECRET or private repository links.
- Do not imply that the recipient is being asked to validate or endorse BAES.
- Agree scope, schedule, confidentiality, attribution, and any fee/terms before asking the person to begin.
- Send individually; do not expose recipients in a group email.
