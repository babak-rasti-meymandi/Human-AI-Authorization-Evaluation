# External Framework Source Register — Initial Scope and Limits

**Status:** Source inventory draft; criterion-level crosswalk not yet completed  
**Access date:** 2026-10-24  
**Purpose:** Establish traceable public sources before mapping scenario criteria. This register is not a finding that any framework does or does not address a criterion.

## E01 — NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0)

- **Publisher:** National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST), U.S. Department of Commerce.
- **Document:** *Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0)*, NIST AI 100-1, authored by Elham Tabassi; published 2023-01-26.
- **Official publication page:** https://www.nist.gov/publications/artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-ai-rmf-10
- **Official full PDF:** https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.100-1
- **Framework landing page:** https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework
- **Use in this study:** Primary public source for an evidence-mapped reading of AI RMF 1.0 against the S01 criteria.
- **Limits:** AI RMF 1.0 is a voluntary, use-case-agnostic risk-management framework. NIST states that AI RMF 1.0 is being revised. This project deliberately evaluates the named 1.0 edition and must not silently substitute a future revision.
- **Reading rule:** Map the actual public framework text to the scenario-derived criterion; do not infer that a broad governance or risk-management outcome itself proves that a particular S01 event was authorized.

## E02 — NIST's public AI RMF crosswalk index

- **Publisher:** NIST AI Resource Center (AIRC).
- **Page:** https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/crosswalks/
- **Use in this study:** Identify and classify publicly available crosswalks, including the NIST-to-ISO/IEC 42001 crosswalk.
- **Important limitation stated by NIST:** Inclusion of a crosswalk does not imply NIST endorsement of the mapped resource or that either resource comprehensively covers the other.
- **Reading rule:** Treat a crosswalk as secondary mapping evidence, not as a replacement for either source's own text. Record who produced the crosswalk, the version it maps, and any draft/final-edition mismatch.

## E03 — NIST AI RMF to ISO/IEC FDIS 42001 crosswalk

- **Title shown by source:** *NIST AI Risk Management Framework to ISO/IEC 42001 Crosswalk*.
- **PDF:** https://airc.nist.gov/docs/NIST_AI_RMF_to_ISO_IEC_42001_Crosswalk.pdf
- **Source status:** The filename identifies the ISO document as FDIS. NIST's crosswalk index lists the ISO-IEC-42001 crosswalk as a Microsoft-provided crosswalk. Hosting/indexing by NIST does not make the mapping a normative ISO publication or imply NIST endorsement.
- **Use in this study:** Contextual aid for locating potentially related topics between AI RMF 1.0 and the draft-final ISO/IEC 42001 text.
- **Limits:** Do not treat the FDIS mapping as proof of exact coverage in the published ISO/IEC 42001:2023 edition. Do not use it to claim full-clause coverage or conformity without verifying against authorized final-edition text.

## E04 — ISO/IEC 42001:2023 official publication information

- **Publisher:** International Electrotechnical Commission (IEC), with ISO/IEC standard designation.
- **Document:** *ISO/IEC 42001:2023 — Information technology — Artificial intelligence — Management system*, Edition 1.0, published 2023-12-18.
- **Official product page:** https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/90574
- **Public ISO preview:** https://www.iso.org/obp/ui/#iso:std:iso-iec-42001:ed-1:v1:en
- **Use in this study:** Verify the final edition's title, scope, publication status, and publicly available informative material.
- **Limits:** The complete normative standard is not available in the public preview. This project does not claim to have reviewed the complete licensed text and must not infer missing requirements from the preview or a third-party crosswalk.
- **Scope note:** ISO/IEC 42001 specifies requirements and guidance for an organizational AI management system (AIMS). It is not interchangeable with NIST AI RMF 1.0, and neither source should be assumed to be a direct event-level authorization-recording instrument without examining the actual text.

## Crosswalk method and reporting rules

1. Use the exact S01 C1–C20 wording and version; do not reconstruct or rename criteria from memory.
2. For each criterion, cite the exact public source section/subcategory/clause and a concise paraphrase of what it addresses.
3. Record the mapping as **explicit**, **indirect**, **not established from the reviewed text**, or **unreviewed / access-limited**. Do not use “absent” where only a limited source was reviewed.
4. Keep these three axes separate: **text coverage**, **scenario evidence sufficiency**, and **ambiguity**.
5. A framework's mention of oversight, accountability, risk, documentation, or traceability does not alone establish that the particular S01 deviation was disclosed, knowingly authorized, or reconstructable from the scenario record.
6. Do not create a composite score or rank frameworks based on this one scenario.
7. Do not reproduce licensed ISO text. Record source title, version/status, URL, access date, and the exact public location used.
